FormulaOne Fleet Management System — WhatsApp attendance integration

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The FormulaOne Fleet Management System (“FMS”) is an internal tool used by our organisation to manage a vehicle fleet and the drivers assigned to it. This policy explains what the WhatsApp part of that system does with information, and who it is for.

Who this applies to

FMS is not a public service. It is used only by our own staff. The WhatsApp integration accepts messages from a small, fixed list of supervisors whose numbers have been registered in the system in advance. Messages from any other number are recorded as unrecognised and are never turned into attendance records.

What we receive

When a registered supervisor sends an attendance register to our WhatsApp Business number, the WhatsApp Business Platform delivers the message to our server. We store:

  • the sender's WhatsApp phone number;
  • the WhatsApp profile name shown by the sender's account;
  • the text of the message;
  • the message identifier and the time it was sent;
  • the delivery payload as received, so a message can be re-read if something goes wrong.

We do not read, request or store contacts, location, photographs, or any message sent to any other recipient.

What we do with it

The message text is read to work out which vehicles and drivers it names and whether each driver was present, absent, on leave or standing in for someone else. The result is held as a draft. A person in our office reviews every draft and confirms it before any attendance is recorded. Nothing is posted to a driver's record automatically.

Where it is kept

All of this is stored in our own database, on our own hosting. It is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or any other third party. It is not used to build advertising profiles. Access is limited to authorised staff who are signed in to FMS.

How long it is kept

Attendance records are kept for as long as our fleet and payroll records require. The original WhatsApp messages behind them are kept so that a recorded attendance can be traced back to what the supervisor actually wrote. Records that are no longer needed are deleted.

Automated messages

The system is set up to receive messages. It does not send marketing or promotional messages. Where a confirmation reply is enabled, it is sent only to the supervisor who sent the register, and only about that register.

Your choices

A supervisor who no longer wishes to send attendance over WhatsApp can ask for their number to be removed from the registered list, after which messages from that number are no longer processed. A supervisor may also ask what has been stored about them, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, subject to the record-keeping obligations above.
